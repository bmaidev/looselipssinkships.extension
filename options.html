AI Chat Guard

Automatically protect against PII being submitted to AI chat services (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Grok, and many more).

Auto-Redact

Automatically redact PII as you type or paste, without clicking the Redact button.

1500 ms

Submit & Upload Guard

Intercept form submissions and file uploads to ensure PII is redacted before data leaves the page.

Reversible Tokenization

Replace PII with unique, numbered tokens ([[EMAIL_1]], [[PERSON_1]]) instead of generic masks. The mapping is kept in memory only, so the AI can reason over the placeholders. Experimental — outbound only: tokens currently appear in the AI's reply; automatic restoration on the way back is a later phase. When off, PII is masked irreversibly (the safer default).

Voice Dictation Detection

Detects when a site captures your microphone for dictation and uploads the audio off-device — spoken PII that would otherwise bypass text redaction. Detection is local and observe-only; no audio is recorded or sent anywhere. Works on any site, with no domain list.

Document Processing

Enable file upload buttons alongside text redaction. Supports Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), HTML, Markdown, PDF, and images. PDF/image redaction uses local OCR (requires vendor setup) or an optional OCR container.
